# First-tier perk icon art direction

## Shared format

- UI format: `UI_Perk_<Name>_Rank_0`
- Final size: 512 x 512 PNG
- Approved style master: `StyleReferences/Approved_Style_Bombardier.png`; match its broad painterly shapes, strong silhouette, restrained surface detail, readable midtones, and simple high-contrast background
- Composition: one original character, knees/waist up, centered and readable
- Rendering: clean stylized 2D game illustration drawn for 512px, using large smooth color masses, clear contour separation, and medium-low detail
- Simplification: few large armor/clothing panels; remove micro-texture entirely, including dense scratches, grime, pores, stitching, tiny rivets, fine hair/fur strands, noise, grain, and repeated surface marks
- Value range: keep the character clearly visible in the midtones; use dark backgrounds without crushed blacks; separate face, clothing, and role prop by clear hue or value differences
- Background: two or three large, bright atmospheric shapes only; no dense smoke texture or busy ruins
- Frame: no decorative frame; thin plain dark edge is acceptable
- Label: none; the artwork fills the full square naturally
- Cast variety: vary exposed face, hair, headgear, body type, pose, and costume silhouette naturally within the KF resource style; do not default to the same sealed helmet or anonymous armored trooper
- Role readability: the defining prop must contrast clearly against clothing in hue and value; never let grenades, tools, medical gear, ammunition, or weapons blend into the costume
- Exclusions: no words, letters, numbers, rank label, perk name, vector pictogram, monochrome skill-card engraving, photorealism, gore, particle clutter, or elaborate card decoration
